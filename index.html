First Steps Upon Waking

Execute these three non-negotiables before checking notifications:

1. Hydrate & Breathe

Drink 500ml of water immediately to kickstart metabolism. Take 5 deep diaphragmatic breaths.

2. Morning Intent

Set your mindset: "Today is a clear slate. I direct my focus only toward what I control."

3. Daylight Exposure

Get 10 minutes of direct sunlight exposure to lock in your circadian sleep-wake rhythm.

Full Day Master Routine

06:30 AM - 08:00 AM

Morning Prime

Hydrate, light physical movement, outdoor sun exposure, and a high-protein breakfast.

08:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Deep Focus Window

Tackle your single highest priority task while cognitive performance is at its peak.

12:00 PM - 01:30 PM

Midday Reset

Eat a nutrient-dense lunch, step away from digital screens, and take a 15-minute walk.

01:30 PM - 05:00 PM

Execution & Admin

Process communications, secondary tasks, collaborative work, and planning.

05:00 PM - 08:00 PM

Personal Time & Exercise

Train physically, engage in hobbies, enjoy dinner, and connect with friends/family.

08:00 PM - 10:30 PM

Night Wind-Down

Dim overhead lighting, eliminate blue light screens, reflect on wins, and rest.
